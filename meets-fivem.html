FENRIR YAKUZA

A WORLD WITH NO LIMITS

Fenrir Yakuza isn't just another version of Los Santos. It is an environment where the world can be expanded, customized, and transformed.

FiveM allows for the use of custom vehicles, clothing, characters, maps, objects, animations, and environments, creating an experience far more flexible than traditional GTA Online.

At Fenrir Yakuza, this freedom translates into an experience where every detail can become part of the player's — and the city's — identity.

🚘

AUTOMOTIVE CULTURE

REAL CAR CULTURE

One of Fenrir Yakuza's major draws is the ability to find cars inspired by real-world brands and models, bringing automotive culture much closer to reality into the FiveM universe.

Vehicle styles & categories

  • JDM
  • European
  • American
  • Supercars
  • Luxury
  • Classics
  • Muscle
  • SUVs
  • Bikes

And it’s not just about picking a car. The experience can involve customization — wheels, colors, liveries, parts, accessories, and various configurations — allowing each vehicle to have its own unique identity.

Within the FiveM ecosystem, custom vehicles can feature everything from visual mods to handling adjustments, liveries, specific parts, and other unique elements.

👕

IDENTITY

YOUR CHARACTER. YOUR STYLE.

At Fenrir Yakuza, players aren't limited to standard GTA characters and clothing. Craft a look that matches your personality, crew, or role in the city.

Customize everything

  • Clothing
  • Hairstyles
  • Beards
  • Accessories
  • Eyewear

  • Footwear
  • Outfits

  • Characters

The result is a truly unique character. Customization systems within the FiveM ecosystem can handle everything from clothing components, props, and hairstyles to colors and other appearance-related elements.

FIVEM RULES

Community standards for sessions and the server

01

Respect Everyone

No harassment, disrespect, or toxic behavior toward players or staff. Treat others the same way you would want to be treated.

02

Community Etiquette

No toxicity: avoid arguments or unnecessary drama in chat. Be patient: allow others to enjoy the game without rushing or interrupting them.

03

No Slurs or Discrimination

Racism, homophobia, or offensive language of any kind is strictly forbidden. Slurs related to race, gender, nationality, or orientation can result in a ban.

04

No Spamming

Don’t flood the chat. Be patient and wait for issues to be resolved before bringing up new ones.

05

In-Game Interaction Rules

No RDM: do not attack or kill other players without proper context. No spam requests: do not repeatedly ask staff for help or items.

06

Server Gameplay Rules

Follow staff instructions at all times. Don’t exploit bugs or glitches for personal gain. Respect the environment — avoid excessive destruction such as mass vehicle explosions.

07

Leadership Authority

Leadership staff may override rules for special events. Leadership reserves the right to modify rules, resolve conflicts, and finalize bans or reports.

08

Ban Process

1st offense: verbal warning · 2nd offense: 2-week ban · 3rd offense: permanent ban. Severe cases (e.g. hacking, mass VDM/RDM) may skip warnings and result in permanent bans or data deletion.
