CENTRAL HUB

OUR DISCORD COMMUNITY

The Bovary Club Society Discord is the central hub of our community. It is where members meet, communicate, organize sessions, share their work, discover new people and stay connected with everything happening within Bovary.

More than a chat server, it works as a complete community platform built around events, media, creativity, communication and social interaction.

📢

SESSIONS & ACTIVITIES

EVENTS & MEETUPS

The Discord is where we organize and announce our community activities. Dedicated channels publish upcoming meets, cruises, sessions and special events.

Members can find

  • Event announcements
  • Date and time
  • Hosts
  • Meeting locations
  • Event themes
  • Activities
  • Special instructions
  • Session updates

Whether it's a large organized meet or a spontaneous late-night session, Discord is where the community comes together to make it happen.

🤖

CUSTOM TOOLS

BOVAS BOT

One of the exclusive tools within our community is BOVAS, our dedicated Discord bot.

BOVAS helps automate and improve different aspects of the server, making the community easier and more interactive to use. Through the bot, members can interact with automated features, reactions and community systems designed specifically for the Bovary Discord environment.

It is one of the elements that makes the server feel less like a standard Discord community and more like a custom-built environment for our members.

📸

MEMORIES & CONTENT

PHOTO & VIDEO SHARING

The Discord gives members dedicated spaces to share their experiences after every session.

  • Photography
  • GTA screenshots
  • Videos
  • Cinematic clips
  • Event photos
  • Vehicle photography
  • Edits

After a session, members can return to the server and share their favorite moments with everyone. It also creates a permanent place where the community's events and memories can be collected and enjoyed by other members.

🎨

CREATORS WELCOME

PERSONAL ART & CREATIONS

Bovary isn't limited to GTA content. Members have dedicated spaces to showcase their own creative work.

  • Digital art
  • Graphic designs
  • Logos
  • Edits
  • Posters
  • Photography
  • Video projects
  • Automotive artwork
  • Personal creations

The Discord gives creators a place to present their work to an audience that understands and appreciates the same interests.

🚗

BUILDS & STYLE

AUTOMOTIVE COMMUNITY

The server also works as a place for automotive enthusiasts to exchange ideas and content.

Members can discuss vehicles, share builds, show their collections, exchange inspiration and discover new styles. From JDM and European cars to American muscle, supercars, classics and motorcycles — there is space for different automotive interests.

💬

HANG OUT

COMMUNITY & SOCIAL

At its core, Discord gives everyone a place to talk — even when there isn't an event happening.

Members can participate in conversations, meet people from different countries, exchange experiences and simply hang out with the community. You don't need to be participating in an event to be part of Bovary. You can join a conversation, share something interesting, show your latest creation or simply meet new people.

🌎
