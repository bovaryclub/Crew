EXPANSION

GROWING THE COMMUNITY

Bovary is not only a single crew — it is a hub for connection. We want to bring friend crews together, increase the flow of meets, expand ideas and create new friendships across the map.

The goal is simple: unite people who enjoy the same style of play — clean cars, good vibes, themed sessions, cruises and nights that feel alive. More crews in the circle means more events, more energy and more possibilities for everyone.

🤝

OPEN CIRCLE

FRIEND CREWS

We actively look for crews that run their own meets and want to share the scene with others.

  • Shared meet nights
  • Cross-crew cruises
  • New ideas & themes
  • More players online
  • Real friendships
  • Bigger presence

You do not need a formal application. Join us, show up at our meets, and be part of the circle. Presence is enough.

✨

TWO WAY STREET

INVITE US TOO

Feel free to invite Bovary to your meets as well.

Community works both ways. If your crew is running a session, cruise or special night, you are welcome to invite us. Shared calendars and open doors make the whole scene stronger — for your members and for ours.

No pressure. No gatekeeping. Just people who love the same game style, meeting more often.

BE PART OF THE CIRCLE

Join the community, show presence at meets, and help the scene grow.
