ABOUT THE CREW

Who they are

Goddess Family is a friendly community crew we share the road with. They bring positive energy, solid meets and a welcoming atmosphere for everyone.

  • Friendly community focus
  • Shared meets with Bovary
  • Good vibes & respect
  • Open to collaboration

THE VIBE

How they roll

Sessions feel family-like: chill, supportive and fun. Perfect for players who want company without pressure.

CAR MEET RULES

Session guidelines

01

No mods (except animations)

NO mods are allowed during the car meet with the exception of animations.

02

No object spawning

Avoid spawning objects during the meet.

03

Overtaking only on highways

Overtaking is allowed only on highways.

04

Respect the host

Respect the host, don't overtake them.

05

No intentional crashing

Do not crash into others intentionally.

06

Keep distance

Maintain a 2-cars distance from others.

07

Lag / connection issues

If facing internet issues/lags, switch to passive mode asap.

08

No weapons or explosions

NO weapons/weaponized vehicles/explosions during the meet. Immediate removal from the session if violated.

09

No spoofed / invisible location

NO spoofed/invisible player location.

10

No teleporting above players

NO teleporting directly above players/meet location.

11

No trolling

NO trolling, respect the event and participants.

12

No copying without permission

Do not copy other player's appearance/car without their permission.
