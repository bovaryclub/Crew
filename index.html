📢 REQUEST INVITATION

Channel · request-invitation

Invitation Request Panel

BOVARY CLUB SOCIETY

Having trouble getting an invite to an active session?

We created the Invitation Request Panel to make requesting an invitation easier. Sometimes you may not have the session Host added to your friends list, or simply may not know who is hosting. With this panel, requesting an invite is quick and simple.

01

Open Discord

Join the Bovary server and go to the 📢┃request-invitation channel.

02

Click “Request Invite”

This automatically creates a session invitation request and notifies the Hosts on Discord.

03

Hosts get notified

Only the Hosts receive the notification — easier for them to see and respond to your request.

🎀

Important: Always make sure there is an active meet/session before requesting an invitation.

⏳ Cooldown: 5 minutes 🔔 Hosts only notified 🤖 Bova’s bot

ABOUT BOVARY

Bovary Club Society is more than a crew — it's a community. A place for people who enjoy cars, cruises, photography, events and, above all, good company.

The best part of GTA Online isn't just the cars we drive, but the people we meet along the way. Whether you belong to an established crew, a new group of friends, or simply enjoy a good session — you're welcome here.
