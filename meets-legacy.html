PRIMARY FORMAT

CAR MEETS

Car Meets are one of Bovary's primary types of gatherings. They usually feature a specific theme that dictates which vehicles participants should bring.

The theme might be based on

  • Make or country of origin
  • Model or category
  • Era
  • Automotive style
  • Color
  • Performance
  • Car culture
  • A specific scenario or concept
  • Cars + outfits combo

The meet typically begins with everyone gathering at a starting point. From there, we might hold car showcases, photo sessions, small-group interactions, voting, special activities, and — most importantly — a group cruise.

The cruise is a key part of the experience. We don't want to simply park the cars in a lot and call it a day. We want to see the cars in motion.

That’s why many meets feature a planned route through San Andreas, passing by various locations on the way to the main event site, or simply visiting the most interesting spots.

🦉

LATE NIGHT SERIES

OWL SESSION

Owl Session was born with a unique concept. It represents our series of gatherings held in the dead of night — smaller, alternative, and more spontaneous.

Owl Sessions can feature

  • Drifting
  • Bikers
  • Police pursuits
  • Mountain trails
  • Supercars
  • Night cruises
  • Experimental themes
  • Quick sessions
  • Any unique late-night idea

That is precisely the identity of Owl Session:
not knowing exactly what to expect from the next session.

01 Motorcycle meetup
02 Police chase
03 Drifting session
04 Mountain exploration

Owl Session is for those who want something more spontaneous, alternative, and outside the traditional format of large meets.

🛣️

THE HEART OF THE SESSION

CRUISES

Cruising is one of the most popular activities within Bovary. For us, driving together is part of the event itself.

Typical session flow

  1. Meeting Point
  2. Cruise
  3. Main Location
  4. Activities
  5. Photos
  6. Final Cruise

The route is often planned to take us through various regions of San Andreas. The goal isn't simply to reach the destination quickly. We want to create the feeling of a genuine car meet crew driving across the city.

  • The cars together
  • The formations
  • The roads
  • The stops
  • The photos
  • The unexpected moments

It is during the cruise that the session often stops feeling like just another event and starts feeling like a true crew experience.
